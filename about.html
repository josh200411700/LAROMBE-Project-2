Who We Are

A Catholic college for a changing world.

At Kabankalan Catholic College, students grow through learning, reflection, leadership, and service. We are committed to helping every learner develop the knowledge, confidence, and compassion to contribute meaningfully to society.

Grounded in Catholic values and inspired by the needs of our community, KCC creates an environment where academic excellence and human development move forward together.
